Hover tooltip that appears after a delay. Wraps any element without changing its layout.

```jsx
<Tooltip content="Outgoing edges represent directional knowledge links">
  <button>→ 4</button>
</Tooltip>
<Tooltip content="ma/alice@3d8f2a1" position="right" delay={200}>
  <Avatar name="alice" size="xs" />
</Tooltip>
```
