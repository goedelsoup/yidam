# Records attest facts about persons, in one direction

Consider an `attests` relationship from record to person, and consider making it the only
edge that carries a claim about a person's dates, places or parentage.

The direction matters more than the name. If a person node can assert its own birth year
without an edge to the record that says so, the corpus can hold a claim nothing sourced —
and the question this domain exists to answer, *which records disagree and where*, becomes
unaskable, because the disagreement has already been resolved into a single field by whoever
typed it last.
