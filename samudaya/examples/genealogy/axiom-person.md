# Person

The unit of genealogical research is a person, and a person is not a record. Two baptismal
entries, a census line and a headstone may all attest one person, or two people who shared a
name in one parish. Which of those is true is the finding — so the person has to be a node
the records point at, rather than a name that lives inside each record separately.

A corpus that models only documents can file them perfectly and still not say who anybody is.
