# A marriage is not an edge between two people

Consider refusing `person -married-to-> person`, and consider the reason rather than the shape.

An edge cannot be attested. A marriage has a date, a place, a document that records it, and
frequently two documents that disagree about the date — and an edge has nowhere to put any of
that. Modelling it as an edge does not simplify the corpus; it moves the evidence somewhere it
cannot be cited and then loses it.

The bootstrap should raise this explicitly if the domain chooses a foundational alignment. In
UFO's vocabulary the bond is a **relator** — an entity whose existence depends on the two
people and which is not either of them — and the ceremony is an **event**. That is a genuine
distinction and it may be more than this corpus needs; the point of surfacing it is that the
answer should be a decision with a recorded reason, not a default.
