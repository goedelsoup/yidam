# Record

A record is a specific artifact created at a specific time by someone with a specific reason
to know — an enumerator walking a district, a clerk copying a register, a descendant
answering a question sixty years later. Its evidentiary weight is a property of the record,
not of the fact it asserts.

This is why it cannot be flattened into the person node. "Born 1849" is not a fact the corpus
holds; it is something a named record asserts, and a second record may assert 1851.
