# Speech event

A recording session: who spoke, who else was present, when, where, in what setting, and under
what agreement it was made.

It is irreducible because almost every property a linguist later wants to condition on lives
here rather than in the transcript — register, audience, whether the speaker was narrating or
answering elicitation prompts, and what the speaker agreed the recording could be used for.
A corpus of transcripts without the events that produced them cannot answer why two forms
differ, and cannot answer whether it is allowed to publish either.
