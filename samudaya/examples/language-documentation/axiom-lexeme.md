# Lexeme

The unit a dictionary entry is about, held separately from the attestations that support it and
from any analysis of it.

Three things get collapsed here and should not be. What was **said** is an attestation, tied to
the elicitation it occurred in. The **lexeme** is the abstraction somebody argued for across
attestations. A segmentation, a gloss or a paradigm assignment is a **claim about** the lexeme.

Attestations do not change and analyses do. Collapsing them is how a corpus loses the ability
to say *this analysis was revised and the data was not* — and it is how a form recorded once, in
one session, comes to look like a settled fact about the language.
