# Etymology

A proposed origin for a lexeme, with the evidence offered for it.

It is a node rather than a field because etymologies are **contested**, and the interesting
state is two proposals with different evidence and no resolution. A field holds one answer,
which means the corpus reports as settled whatever the last editor believed.

A domain where every etymology reads as settled is visibly wrong to any practitioner, which is
what makes this the easiest place in the corpus to see why claim tags are structural rather
than decorative.
