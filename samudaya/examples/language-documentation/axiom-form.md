# Form

An attested stretch of language — a word, a construction, a paradigm cell — as it occurred,
tied to the speech event it occurred in.

Separate from the analysis of it. The form is what was said; a segmentation, a gloss or a
paradigm assignment is a claim about the form, and claims get revised while attestations do
not. Collapsing the two is how a corpus loses the ability to say *this analysis changed and
the data did not*.
