# Elicitation

A recording session: who spoke, who else was present, when, where, in what setting, and under
what agreement it was made.

Irreducible because almost every property a linguist later conditions on lives here rather than
in the transcript — register, audience, and **whether the speaker was answering prompts or
narrating**, which is not a detail. Elicited and naturalistic data have different evidential
weight for almost every claim anyone wants to make, and a corpus that cannot tell them apart
will average them.

It is also where permission lives. A corpus of transcripts without the sessions that produced
them cannot answer why two forms differ, and cannot answer whether it is allowed to publish
either.
