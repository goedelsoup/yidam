# A recording's access conditions bind everything derived from it

If a speech event was recorded under conditions restricting who may hear it or what may be
published from it, those conditions apply to the transcripts, the examples quoted in an
analysis, and any bundle or index this repository publishes.

The bootstrap must not scaffold anything that publishes corpus content by default, and the
derived repository's private paths must be settled at genesis rather than added later. A
restriction discovered after publication is not a restriction that was honoured.
