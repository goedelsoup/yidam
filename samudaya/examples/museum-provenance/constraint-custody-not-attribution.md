# This corpus records custody, not authorship

Who held the object, when, and on what evidence — not who made it, whether it is genuine, or
what it is worth.

Attribution is a live scholarly argument with its own literature and its own standards of
evidence, and a corpus that mixes the two produces a graph where "the Rembrandt" and "the
painting sold in 1923" are the same node under two contested descriptions. Scope it out at
genesis; a later corpus can cite this one.
