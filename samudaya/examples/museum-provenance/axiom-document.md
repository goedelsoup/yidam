# Document

The thing that attests a transfer: a bill of sale, an auction catalogue entry, a shipping
record, an export licence, a letter, an accession register line.

It is irreducible for the same reason it is in every other evidentiary domain — the transfer is
what happened and the document is why anyone believes it, and collapsing the two leaves the
corpus asserting custody with no way to say on what basis.

It is also where the negative results live. A search that found nothing is a document: it has
an author, a date, a scope, and it is the only thing that distinguishes an unexamined gap from
an examined one.
