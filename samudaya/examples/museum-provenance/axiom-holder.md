# Holder

A person or institution that held the object, as the documents name them.

Separate from the custody event, because the interesting questions are about a holder across
objects and across time — what else passed through this dealer, whether two spellings in two
catalogues are one firm — and a name written inside a transfer record cannot be asked any of
them.

Identity here is a claim rather than a given, and it is frequently the whole difficulty: a
dealer operating under three business names across two countries is one holder or three
depending on an argument somebody has to make and write down.
